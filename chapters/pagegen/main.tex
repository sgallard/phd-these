\section{Conclusion}

This chapter introduced \methodNameGeneral, a multi-objective evolutionary framework for automated newspaper page generation that jointly resolves template assignment and two-dimensional packing across four editorial objectives ($\objone$-$\objfour$). Two variants were proposed: \methodNameUniform, based on standard NSGA-III, autonomously explores the full Pareto frontier without requiring historical design preferences, while \methodName, based on Reference-Point NSGA-III, steers the search toward aspiration points derived from historical layouts to align more closely with editorial practice. 
Both share a sequence-preserving backtracking packing decoder that deterministically converts a chromosome into a feasible layout.

Empirically, this joint optimization paradigm proved decisively superior to the sequential, decoupled pipelines of baselines: \methodNameUniform\ discovers Pareto-dominating solutions in the large majority of runs across all complexity strata, and outperforms the ablated single-objective baseline $\baseline{5}$ in hypervolume across both the full objective space and its reduced 3D subspace. 

Between the two variants, \methodName\ is the one best suited for production use: rather than merely covering the objective space more broadly, it compresses the non-dominated frontier by an order of magnitude relative to \methodNameUniform, while concentrating up to $51.4\%$ more of its solutions within the empirically defined Acceptability Envelope (AE), turning dozens of candidate layouts into a compact, editor-ready proposal.

In summary, \methodNameGeneral\ resolves the central objective of this thesis: automating the \melody web-to-print workflow, generating a complete print page from web articles without manual template assignment or placement (\secref{sec:multi-objective-page-generation}). It achieves this by directly consuming the page-aware compatibility scores produced by \neuralmethod\ (\secref{sec:gwes}) as its editorial-style objective $\objtwo$, guiding the evolutionary search toward historically coherent template assignments. 

Two directions remain open for extending this method. 
First, incorporating advertisement placement, already discussed as an architectural extension in \secref{sec:pagegen-discussion}.
Second, scaling beyond single-page composition toward full-edition generation, jointly sequencing and assigning articles across the multiple pages of an issue rather than treating each page in isolation. This latter direction is revisited in \secref{sec:limitations-multipage}.

\subsection*{Deployment}

\methodNameGeneral\ was deployed successfully on a pre-production server within \melody's infrastructure, exposed as a Python REST API together with a new \melody graphical interface, and is currently serving as a pilot functionality for the \laprovence press group. It is currently undergoing in-depth testing with \laprovence, while new implementations are being evaluated jointly with the \publihebdos press group.
