# Context 

## Press Business evolution

### What is the situation now?
- Before: the print only. Revenues through ads and direct sell
- Digital era: Web versions became main revenue, because ad companies prefered to go there. The print decay. 
- Social network incursion: Web started decaying because people preferred to read through social networks. 
- AI era: point to google-ai-and-press-article.md, an article from a few weeks ago. Now the web is decaying even more, and all the press is in danger

### Given this, is it worth to continue producing the print in this context?
YES, because
- Is still source of revenue through subscriptions, or in some specific countries
- Layout has its benefits related to spatial arrangement, entry points that guide the scan/read, etc.
- In the current status of AI and the digital press (previous section), publishers look for special supplementary or "premium" products that cannot be replaced by genAI. The print and its layout proposal is an interesting alterantive, so a "revival of the print" it might be a trend in the future.



#### More general, the print is a layout, regardless if is in paper or not
- The important part is the 2D layout where the news are displayed, regardless if is in print paper or not
- this also brings another advantage: if the layout can be also digital, then an interesting use case are dynamic, multimodal editions with all the digital features without loosing the print layout benefits.
*From now on, in this thesis, print is used as an equivalent of print 2D layout or digital 2D layout*

So the answer is yes, it is worth to produce the print **as soon as products costs are optimized, which is exactly the problem in the current press industry** because
- Designing is expensive
- Printing is expensive
- and distributing is expensive
